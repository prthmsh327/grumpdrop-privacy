Grump Drop – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Grump Drop is a physics puzzle game published by Mindset Clarity. This policy explains what information the game handles.

Information we collect

Grump Drop does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal information. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Information stored on your device

Your level progress (stars earned) and your sound on/off setting are saved only in a file on your own device so you can continue where you left off. This file never leaves your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its app data deletes it.

Permissions

The game uses the vibrate permission only to give short haptic feedback when you tap shapes. It does not use the internet, camera, microphone, location, contacts or storage outside its own app folder.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
